## What you will find here

BookSpaceB612 started as a place to keep my thoughts on the books I have read — what I liked about them, what stayed with me, and what I was still turning over in my head after the last page.

## Why B612

B612 is the Little Prince's asteroid. A small planet with room for one rose, three volcanoes and one person who loves it.

The blog carries that name after a friend who loved *The Little Prince* and who kept reminding me that it is never too late to start something we have always wanted to try. That it is always worth giving time to what we enjoy.

She is no longer here. The blog exists because she was right.

> *"All grown-ups were once children. But only few of them remember it."*

## How this works

You will find reviews, the sentences that made me stop, and a rating on four axes I call the **B612 Compass**:

- **Style** — how the book is written
- **Story** — how the plot works
- **Depth** — the depth of its ideas
- **Echo** — what stays with me after finishing it

It is not about points, it is about the shape those four values draw together. A book can be beautifully written and still leave nothing behind. Another can have a weaker story and yet stay in my head for a long time.

> *"It is only with the heart that one can see rightly; what is essential is invisible to the eye."*
